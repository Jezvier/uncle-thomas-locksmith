# Competitor analysis — Singapore locksmiths (Oct 2026)

Sites reviewed: First Locks, 24/7 Locksmith Singapore, Vincent Locksmith, Ketch, Botak Locksmiths, Locksmith in SG. Also the listicles that dominate "locksmith Singapore" results (MoneySmart, sbo.sg, The MoneyBees) and Carousell.

| Competitor | What they do well | Weakness we exploit |
|---|---|---|
| **Vincent Locksmith** | Huge review count (~700+ on Google), 10+ town pages, a page per job type, testimonial pages, "featured in" pages linking to listicles | Very heavy pages (2MB+), no clear price list, H1 missing on homepage |
| **First Locks** | "From $39" price in title, fixed-price messaging, warranty page, emergency service page | Mega-menu clutter, thin generic copy, also serves Johor Bahru (diluted Singapore focus) |
| **24/7 Locksmith SG** | Price in title, "30-min response" claim, rich schema incl. AggregateRating, address | Generic copy, no founder story or credentials |
| **Ketch** | Rates page, UKLA certification, very large blog targeting every "locksmith near me"/town variant | Blog is mass-produced and repetitive; little personality |
| **Botak** | Short, punchy, phone in title | No schema, no prices, 2 internal links |

## Our edge (built into the site)

1. **Founder authority** — apprentice since 1998, US-trained safesmith, safes on ships and across Asia, the trade's "Shifu". No competitor has a story this strong. Used on About, homepage, safe and digital lock pages, plus Person schema.
2. **Safe opening** — almost nobody ranks for "safe opening Singapore" with credentials. Dedicated page + guide.
3. **Trust signals competitors bury** — ID verification, price before work, no-expiry warranty, honest "we don't repair". Repeated on every page.
4. **Clean, fast pages** — Lighthouse 97–100 vs competitors' heavy WordPress builds.
5. **Prices published** (like First Locks / Ketch), plus an anti-overcharging guide.
6. **Genuine town pages** — local estates/MRT/common jobs per town, not copy-paste.

## What competitors have that we still need (off-site)

- **Google reviews** — the #1 gap. Vincent's review volume is why they win "near me". Ask every customer.
- **Listicle features** — Vincent links to 10+ "best locksmiths" articles. Email MoneySmart, sbo.sg, The MoneyBees, SingaReviews, etc.
- **Directory profiles** — Carousell services, Facebook page, Yellow Pages SG. Add each URL to `SAME_AS`.
