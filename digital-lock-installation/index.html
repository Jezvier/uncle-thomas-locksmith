Digital Lock Installation in Singapore

Why installation matters

Most digital lock problems we're called out for start with a poor installation — a misaligned strike plate, a cable pinched in the door, or a lock that doesn't suit the door. A digital lock is only as good as how it's fitted. With 28+ years of opening and fitting locks, Uncle Thomas knows exactly what makes a lock last in Singapore's humid weather.

What we install

  • HDB main door digital locks — including replacing an old mechanical lock.
  • HDB gate digital locks — rim and mortise types for metal gates.
  • Condo and landed home locks — main doors, side doors and gates.
  • Office and shop locks — keypad and card access for small businesses. See commercial locksmith.

We work with all brands, including Samsung, Yale, Philips, Igloohome, Solity, Gateman, Kaadas, Häfele, Aqara, Schlage. Bought your own lock online? We'll install it. Not sure which to get? Tell us your door and budget and we'll recommend one that fits.

How it works

  1. WhatsApp 9224 9224 a photo of your door (outside and inside edge) and the lock you want.

  3. We install, test every function, and set up your PIN, fingerprints and cards with you.
  4. You get our no-expiry warranty on the installation.

Our warranty: Every lock replacement and installation comes with our warranty — with no expiry date.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I install a digital lock on my HDB main door?

Yes. Digital locks are common on HDB main doors and gates. We check your door's thickness and existing cut-outs so the lock fits properly.

Can you install a lock I bought online?

Yes. Send us a photo of the lock and your door first so we can confirm it will fit.

How long does installation take?

Most installations are completed in one visit. We test every function and set up your codes and fingerprints before we leave.

Is there a warranty?

Yes — every installation comes with our warranty, with no expiry date.

Do you repair digital locks?

No, we don't do repairs. If an existing lock is faulty we can replace it with a new one.
