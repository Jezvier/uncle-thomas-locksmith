Car lockouts we handle

  • Keys locked inside the car or boot.
  • Remote key battery dead and the door won't open.
  • Car locked at a carpark, petrol station or roadside — anywhere in Singapore.
  • Cars, vans and light commercial vehicles.

Non-destructive unlocking

We use professional car entry tools that open the door without damaging the paintwork, rubber seals or windows. It's much cheaper than breaking a window — and safer than trying a coat hanger yourself, which can damage the door's wiring and airbag sensors.

Proof of ownership: Before unlocking, we check that the car is yours — for example your NRIC against the vehicle log card, or a rental or company car agreement.
