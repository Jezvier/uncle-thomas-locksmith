Letterbox Lock Replacement in Singapore

Lost the key to your HDB or condo letterbox, or the lock is broken? We open it and fit a new lock with new keys — quickly, with proof of residence checked first.

We can help if…

  • You lost your letterbox key.
  • The lock is jammed, rusty or broken.
  • You just moved in and want to make sure no one else has a key.
  • Someone tampered with your letterbox lock.

How it works

  1. WhatsApp 9224 9224 with your block and unit, and a photo of the letterbox.
  2. We confirm the price and arrange a time — letterboxes don't need a midnight call-out, so we'll come at a time that suits you.
  3. We check your proof of residence, open the letterbox and fit a new lock with new keys.

Why we check ID: A letterbox holds bank letters and IDs. For your security, we always check proof of residence or ownership before opening anything — for example NRIC with your address, a tenancy agreement or utility bill, or the car's log card.

Price

Letterbox lock replacement starts from S$60, including a new lock and keys. Every lock replacement and installation comes with our warranty — with no expiry date. See all prices.

Frequently Asked Questions

I lost my HDB letterbox key. What should I do?

WhatsApp us your block and a photo of the letterbox. We'll open it and fit a new lock with new keys after checking your proof of residence.

Do you make duplicate letterbox keys?

No, we don't do key duplication. We replace the lock and give you a new set of keys.

How much does letterbox lock replacement cost?

From S$60, including the new lock and keys.
