Damage-free first

With 28+ years in the trade, Uncle Thomas opens most mechanical and digital locks without drilling or forcing the door. If a lock is badly worn or broken and can't be opened cleanly, we explain your options and the price before doing anything else. If the lock then needs changing, we can replace it on the spot — and every replacement comes with our no-expiry warranty.

Why we ask for ID: For your security, we always check proof of residence or ownership before opening anything — for example NRIC with your address, a tenancy agreement or utility bill, or the car's log card. It protects you — a locksmith who opens any door without checking is a risk to everyone.

Frequently Asked Questions

How fast can an emergency locksmith reach me?

We set off as soon as you call, 24/7. Arrival time depends on where you are and traffic — we'll give you an honest estimate on the phone.

Will you damage my door?

We use damage-free methods wherever possible, and most lockouts are opened without any damage. If a lock can't be opened cleanly, we explain the options and price before doing anything.

What do I need to show before you open the door?

Proof that you live there — for example your NRIC with the address, a tenancy agreement or a utility bill. This protects you and your home.

Do you charge more at night?

Lockouts start from S$80 from 9am to 6pm and from S$120 after hours, weekends and public holidays. You'll always know the price before we start.

Do you open locks for tenants?

Yes — as long as you can show you live there, for example with a tenancy agreement or a letter with your name and the address.
