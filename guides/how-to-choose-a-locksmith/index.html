How to Choose a Locksmith in Singapore — and Avoid Overcharging

By Uncle Thomas, locksmith since 1998 · Updated October 2026

📞 Call 9224 9224💬 WhatsApp Us

When you're locked out, you want help fast — and that's exactly when people get overcharged. Here's how to pick a locksmith you can trust, from someone who has been in the trade since 1998.

1. Get the total price before anyone sets off

Describe your lock (or send a photo) and ask: "What will the total be?" A good locksmith gives a clear figure, or a narrow range and what would change it. Be wary of anyone who only quotes a tiny "call-out fee".

2. Be careful with very low "from" prices

An advert that seems far cheaper than everyone else can be a sign of bait pricing — a low starting price that grows at your door. Compare a few locksmiths' price lists. Ours is published openly on our prices page.

3. Check real reviews

Look at the locksmith's Google reviews — especially recent ones that mention price and honesty, not just speed.

4. Expect an ID check — it's a good sign

An honest locksmith will ask for proof that you live there before opening the door. If a locksmith opens any door without checking, they'd do the same for anyone — including someone trying to get into your home.

5. Ask about damage-free entry

Most lockouts can be opened without drilling. If a locksmith goes straight for the drill, ask why — and ask the price of a replacement lock before they start.

6. Ask about a warranty

If a lock is replaced or installed, ask what warranty comes with it. We cover every replacement and installation with a warranty that has no expiry date.

7. Get a receipt

Ask for a receipt or invoice showing what was done and what you paid. If you have a dispute, the Consumers Association of Singapore (CASE) can advise you.

Quick Answers

What's a fair price for a locksmith in Singapore?

For a standard daytime lockout, published prices in 2026 generally start from around S$80–S$90, with higher rates after hours. Digital locks, safes and replacements cost more. Always confirm the total before work begins.

Should a locksmith ask for my ID?

Yes. Checking proof of residence or ownership is a sign of a trustworthy locksmith.
