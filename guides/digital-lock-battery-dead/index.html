Digital Lock Battery Dead or Won't Open? Try These First

By Uncle Thomas, locksmith since 1998 · Updated October 2026

📞 Call 9224 9224💬 WhatsApp Us

Digital locks are convenient — until they won't open. Most lockouts we're called to have one of a few causes. Here's what to check first, as the lock's owner. (Every brand is a little different, so check your manual if you have it.)

1. Flat battery? Use the emergency power point

Most digital locks warn you for weeks before the batteries run out. If they've died completely, many models — including popular Samsung, Yale and Philips locks — have an emergency power terminal on the outside, often for a 9V battery or a USB-C cable. Hold the power source to the terminal, then enter your code as normal. Once inside, replace all the batteries with fresh ones of the same type.

2. Use the mechanical backup key

Many digital locks have a hidden keyhole behind a small cover. If you kept the backup key that came with the lock, it can open the door even without power.

3. Is the lock in privacy or double-lock mode?

If someone inside switched on privacy mode (or "double lock"), the lock may refuse all codes from outside. If anyone is home, ask them to switch it off from the inside.

4. Wrong code too many times?

After several wrong attempts, most locks freeze the keypad for a few minutes. Wait for the time-out to end, then try once carefully.

5. Keypad wet or not lighting up?

Rain, corridor washing or condensation can stop a keypad responding. Gently dry the keypad and wait a few minutes. Don't spray anything into the lock.

6. Still stuck? Don't force it

Pulling hard on the handle or prying the lock can break the motor or the door. If none of the above works, call a locksmith who works with digital locks. Uncle Thomas has developed his own damage-free methods for opening faulty digital locks of all brands — see digital lock opening (from S$150).

Tip: change your digital lock batteries as soon as the low-battery warning starts — and keep a 9V battery in your car or bag if your lock supports emergency power.

Quick Answers

How long do digital lock batteries last?

Typically several months to about a year, depending on the lock and how often it's used. Replace them as soon as the low-battery warning appears.

Will a flat battery reset my codes?

Usually not — most locks keep their codes when the batteries run out. Check your lock's manual to be sure.
