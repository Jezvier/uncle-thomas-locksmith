Locked Out of Your HDB Flat? What to Do, Step by Step

By Uncle Thomas, locksmith since 1998 · Updated October 2026

📞 Call 9224 9224💬 WhatsApp Us

Getting locked out is stressful, especially late at night or with kids or elderly family inside. Here's what Uncle Thomas — a locksmith since 1998 — recommends, in order.

1. Stay calm and check for anyone at risk

If a child, an elderly person or someone unwell is alone inside, or you smell gas or see smoke, call 995 immediately. The SCDF handles emergencies where lives may be at risk.

2. Check the easy options

  • Is anyone else home or nearby with a key — family, a helper or a flatmate?
  • Did you leave a spare key with a relative or neighbour?
  • Is the gate locked but the door open, or the other way round? Different locks need different help.
  • Digital lock? Try the steps in our digital lock guide — a flat battery is the most common cause.

3. Don't force the door

Kicking the door, prying the gate or poking the lock with a card or wire usually breaks the lock — turning a simple lockout into a lock replacement. HDB main doors are also fire-rated, so a damaged door can be costly to fix.

4. Call a locksmith — and ask the price first

When you call, ask for the total price before anyone sets off. A good locksmith will ask what type of lock you have (a photo on WhatsApp helps) and give you a clear figure. For reference, our lockouts start from S$80 during the day and S$120 after hours — see our price list.

5. Have your ID ready

A trustworthy locksmith will check that you live there before opening the door. Have something showing your name and the address — NRIC, a tenancy agreement, a bill or a letter. If it's inside the flat, tell the locksmith: they can usually verify it once the door is open, in front of you.

6. Prevent it next time

  • Leave a spare key with a trusted relative or neighbour.
  • Consider a digital lock — no keys to forget (but change the batteries when it warns you).
  • Replace stiff or worn locks before they fail — see lock replacement.

Quick Answers

Can the police or SCDF open my door if I'm locked out?

The SCDF responds to emergencies where life or property is at risk (call 995). For an ordinary lockout, you'll need a locksmith.

Does HDB help if I'm locked out?

HDB doesn't provide lockout services. Call a locksmith, and make sure they check your proof of residence.
