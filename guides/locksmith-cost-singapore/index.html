What the wider market charges

Published starting prices from Singapore locksmiths in 2026 generally put a standard daytime lockout somewhere around S$80–S$90, with after-hours lockouts higher. Some locksmiths advertise much lower "call-out" or "from" prices; these often don't include the actual work. Digital lock opening, safes and lock replacements typically cost more than a basic lockout. Prices change, so always confirm with the locksmith you call.

What makes a locksmith price go up?

1. Time of day

Most locksmiths charge more after office hours, late at night, on weekends and on public holidays. With us, that's the difference between S$80 and S$120 for a lockout.

2. Type of lock

A standard HDB mortise lock is usually quicker to open than a high-security deadbolt, a double-locked door, or a faulty digital lock. Safes vary the most of all.

3. Whether parts are needed

If the lock is broken, badly worn, or a key has snapped and damaged the cylinder, the lock may need replacing. The new lock and fitting are added to the price — a good locksmith shows you the options and the cost first.

4. Damage-free vs destructive entry

Damage-free opening takes skill but saves you the cost of a new lock. Drilling is sometimes necessary, but then a replacement is almost always needed too.

5. Location and access

Most locksmiths cover the whole island at the same rates, but access issues — condo guardhouses, office buildings, carparks — can add time.

Costs that catch people out

  • Call-out fees quoted on their own, with the real work charged on top.
  • "Special tool" charges added on site.
  • Unnecessary drilling followed by an expensive replacement lock.
  • Cheap replacement locks that fail again within months.

Is it cheaper to replace the lock or keep calling a locksmith?

If a lock keeps jamming, or you've been locked out more than once by the same lock, replacing it is usually cheaper in the long run. A main door or gate lock replacement starts from S$100 with us — often not much more than a second lockout — and comes with our no-expiry warranty.

Many home insurance policies in Singapore cover replacing locks and keys, either as a standard benefit or an optional add-on, typically up to S$500–S$1,000. Check your policy, and keep the locksmith's receipt in case you need to claim.

Ways to keep the cost down

  • Call during the day if it's not urgent — daytime rates are lower.
  • Send a photo so the locksmith brings the right tools the first time.
  • Try the simple fixes first — a spare key, emergency power or the backup key.
  • Don't force the lock — damage turns an opening into a replacement.
  • Replace worn locks early — before they lock you out.
  • Book non-urgent jobs like letterbox locks and lock upgrades for a convenient time.

Paying and receipts

Ask how you can pay before the job starts, and always get a receipt showing the business name, the work done and the amount. You'll need it for warranty claims and, in some cases, insurance.

Prices for businesses

Commercial jobs — office lockouts, shop shutters, lock changes when staff leave, cabinets and business safes — are quoted upfront because every site is different. The same factors apply: time of day, lock type and parts. For offices in managed buildings, arranging building access in advance can shorten the job. We provide proper invoices for company accounts.
