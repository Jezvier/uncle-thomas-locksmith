From apprentice to master locksmith

Uncle Thomas started in 1998 as an apprentice under his uncle, learning the trade the traditional way — one lock, one door, one customer at a time. Years of hands-on work across Singapore's HDB flats, condos, shophouses and offices built the foundation he still relies on today.

Trained in safesmithing in the United States

Wanting to master the hardest locks in the trade, he spent a year in the United States training in safesmithing — the specialist skill of opening and servicing safes and vaults. It's a skill few locksmiths in Singapore have. See our safe opening service.

Trusted overseas — on land and at sea

That expertise has taken Uncle Thomas far beyond Singapore. He has been called in to open safes for multinational companies and on board ships and cruise liners, with jobs in Thailand, Malaysia, China, Hong Kong and more.

Keeping up with digital locks

As homes moved from keys to keypads, Uncle Thomas kept learning. He has developed his own damage-free techniques for opening faulty and locked digital locks — so you get back in without needing to replace an expensive lock. See digital lock opening.

Why they call him "Shifu"

In the trade, Uncle Thomas is known as the "Shifu" — the master other locksmiths turn to when a job gets difficult. Today he leads a small team of locksmiths, and he still personally handles the toughest jobs.

How we work

  • 24/7, islandwide — we set off as soon as you call.
  • Price before work — upfront starting prices, and your final price confirmed before we begin.
  • ID verified — For your security, we always check proof of residence or ownership before opening anything — for example NRIC with your address, a tenancy agreement or utility bill, or the car's log card.
  • Warranty — Every lock replacement and installation comes with our warranty — with no expiry date.
  • Specialists — we focus on opening doors, digital locks and safes, and on lock replacement and installation. We don't do lock repairs, key duplication or key cutting, except cloning car key fobs.
