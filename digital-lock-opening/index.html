Common digital lock problems we solve

  • Battery went flat and the emergency power option didn't work.
  • Forgotten PIN or fingerprint no longer recognised.
  • Keypad dead or not lighting up — often after rain or a corridor wash.
  • Lock jammed or motor not turning, even with the right code.
  • Locked from the inside — privacy or double-lock mode left on.
  • Lost the backup key for the mechanical override.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can you open a digital lock without damaging it?

In most cases, yes. Uncle Thomas has developed his own damage-free techniques. If a lock is completely broken we'll explain the options before doing anything.

Which digital lock brands do you open?

All brands — including Samsung, Yale, Philips, Igloohome, Solity, Gateman, Kaadas, Häfele, Aqara, Schlage.

My digital lock battery is flat. What should I do?

Most digital locks have an emergency power point (often a 9V battery terminal) or a backup key. If those don't work, call or WhatsApp 9224 9224 and we'll open it for you.

Do you repair digital locks?

No — we don't do repairs. We open the lock, and if it's faulty we can replace it with a new one, covered by our no-expiry installation warranty.

How much does it cost to open a digital lock?

From S$150. Send a photo on WhatsApp for an exact price before we come.
