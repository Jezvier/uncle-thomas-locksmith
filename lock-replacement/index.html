Door & Gate Lock Replacement in Singapore

When to change your locks

  • You moved into a new home — you never know how many old keys are out there.
  • Keys lost or stolen.
  • A tenant or helper has moved out.
  • The lock is stiff, loose or jams — a worn lock can fail and lock you out.
  • After a break-in or attempted break-in.
  • Upgrading security — or switching to a digital lock.

Locks we replace

  • HDB main door locks — mortise locks, cylinder locks, deadbolts and lever handles.
  • HDB metal gate locks — hook bolts, padlocks and gate locksets.
  • Room, toilet and bedroom door locks and knobs.
  • Condo, landed and office door locks.
  • Letterbox and mailbox locks.

Our warranty: Every lock replacement and installation comes with our warranty — with no expiry date.

Replace, don't repair

We don't do lock repairs or key duplication. If a lock is faulty, we open it and replace it, so the problem doesn't come back. A new lock is usually more reliable, often not much more expensive than a repair, and comes with our warranty.

Frequently Asked Questions

Should I change my locks after moving into a new flat?

Yes, it's a good idea. Previous owners, tenants or contractors may still have keys.

Do you repair locks?

No. We replace faulty locks with new ones, which are more reliable and come with our no-expiry warranty.

Can you replace my lock if I'm locked out?

Yes — we open the door first, then replace the lock in the same visit if needed.

Is there a warranty on replacement locks?

Yes. Every lock replacement comes with our warranty, with no expiry date.
