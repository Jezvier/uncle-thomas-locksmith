Lock tips for Sembawang homes and businesses

Sembawang is quieter than many towns, which makes it easy for us to reach at night — but roads near the coast and Sembawang Park can be hard to find in the dark, so a pinned location on WhatsApp helps. In the newer Canberra estates, digital locks are common, and the advice is the same as elsewhere: change batteries at the first warning and know where your backup key is. If you lose your keys, consider changing the main door cylinder, especially if anything with your address went missing too.
