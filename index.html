Frequently Asked Questions

Are you really available 24/7?

Yes. Uncle Thomas Locksmith works 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, including weekends and public holidays, anywhere in Singapore.

How fast can you reach me?

We set off as soon as you call. Arrival time depends on your location and traffic — we'll give you an honest estimate on the phone. Call or WhatsApp 9224 9224.

How much does a locksmith cost in Singapore?

Our lockouts start from S$80 (9am–6pm) and S$120 after hours, weekends and public holidays. Car lockouts start from S$100 and digital lock opening from S$150. Your final price is always confirmed before any work begins. See our full price list.

Do you check that I live there before opening the door?

Yes. For your security, we always check proof of residence or ownership before opening anything — for example NRIC with your address, a tenancy agreement or utility bill, or the car's log card. It's how we keep every home safe.

Which areas in Singapore do you cover?

All of Singapore — Central, East, West, North and North-East. See areas we serve.

Is there a warranty?

Every lock replacement and installation comes with our warranty — with no expiry date.

Do you do key duplication or lock repairs?

No. We specialise in opening doors, digital locks and safes, and in lock replacement and installation. Faulty locks are replaced, not repaired.
